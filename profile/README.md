# Hexio Labs

**Web design and AI automation agency in Vancouver**

Hexio Labs builds websites, automation, local SEO and custom software for small businesses in Vancouver and the Lower Mainland. Free first call.

Hexio Labs is a web design and AI automation agency in Vancouver, BC, working with small businesses across the Lower Mainland and Canada. We build websites that bring in enquiries, AI receptionists and chatbots that answer calls and messages around the clock, automations that handle quotes, bookings and follow-ups, local SEO that gets businesses found on Google Maps and named by ChatGPT, and custom software when an off-the-shelf app doesn't fit. Every project starts with a free call and a written plan with a fixed price, so the cost is clear before work begins. We write in plain language, not tech talk, and stay on call after launch. Recent work includes a real estate team website with listings and search, a mortgage broker site, a medical aesthetics clinic site and a luxury home builder site. Hexio Labs is the trading name of Infocus Creations Corp, founded by Ishan Sachdeva.

## Services

1. Websites for small businesses
2. AI receptionists and website chatbots
3. Automation for quotes, bookings and follow-ups
4. Local SEO, Google Business Profile management and AI search
5. Custom software and online stores
